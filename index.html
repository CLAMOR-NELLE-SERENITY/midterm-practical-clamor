What is Roblox?

Roblox is the ultimate virtual universe that lets you create, share experiences with friends, and be anything you can imagine.

Key Features

  • Millions of Games: Play everything from epic role-playing adventures to competitive obstacle courses.
  • Cross-Platform Play: Connect with friends across PC, mobile, console, and VR devices.
  • Avatar Customization: Personalize your look with thousands of hats, shirts, faces, gear, and items.

Developer Community

Anyone can become a creator on Roblox! Powered by Roblox Studio and Lua scripting, aspiring developers learn valuable game design and coding skills while sharing their creations with a global audience.

Community Highlights

Robux: The official virtual currency used to purchase avatar upgrades, game passes, and developer items.

Roblox Studio: The free game engine tool used by millions of creators to build custom interactive experiences.
